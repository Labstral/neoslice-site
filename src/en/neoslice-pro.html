neoForge

Draw the part that doesn't exist.

A full 3D modeler, launched from neoSlice. Nothing to learn: place a shape, then stretch, drill and round it right on the part, with the mouse.

  • Cube, cylinder, sphere, cone, torus
  • Gears and frames
  • Threaded holes M2 to M12
  • Nut pockets
  • Live fillets and chamfers
  • Plane cut
  • Snapping measuring tool
  • Millimeters or inches

When the part is ready, it goes to neoSlice in one click, with the right print settings.

Installs in one click from neoSlice. Windows and macOS.

Oen

Your local 3D printing expert.

An AI assistant that runs right on your computer, offline. No data sent anywhere. It knows your printer, your settings and the analysis of the current part, and helps with troubleshooting, calibration and maintenance, for every brand. A Thinking mode kicks in for tough questions.

AI Diagnostic

One photo. The defect. The fix.

Take a photo of a failed print. neoSlice identifies the defect, elephant's foot, stringing, under-extrusion, warping, layer shift, and suggests corrections to apply straight to your settings. Already know the defect? Pick it from the list, no photo needed.

Multicolor export

Your colors, counted to the gram.

After exporting a multicolor file, neoSlice calculates filament weight per color, colors the 3D preview, matches your spools and updates your stock automatically after printing.
