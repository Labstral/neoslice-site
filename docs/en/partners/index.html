Reach a community that prints, tests and recommends.

The Bambu Lab pour les nuls group and neoSlice bring together equipped French-speaking makers who ask for advice before they buy. Our media kit covers the audience, engagement and possible partnership formats.

Partnership formats

  • Product test: a printer, filament or accessory tested in real conditions, with a detailed review published in the group.
  • Sponsored post: a presentation of your brand or a new product, clearly labeled as a partnership.
  • Giveaway: a giveaway run with your brand.
  • Integration into neoSlice: your filaments or printers in the catalog, with independently verified settings, and no paid ranking or recommendation.
