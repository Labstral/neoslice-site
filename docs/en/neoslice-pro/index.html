neoGen

The exact part, generated.

Stop hunting the web for a model that almost fits. Choose the object, set its dimensions, and neoSlice generates it at the right size, ready to print.

Keychains. Cable clips sized to the exact diameter. Gaskets. Brackets. Screws and nuts. Photo frames. QR codes. Business cards.

Raised, engraved or flush text. Your colors. Every part is designed to print without supports.

neoForge

Draw the part that doesn't exist.

A full 3D modeler, launched from neoSlice. Place a shape, stretch it with the mouse, drill, hollow, round the edges. Nothing to learn before you start: everything is adjusted right on the part, with arrows and circles you grab with the mouse.

Cube, cylinder, sphere, cone, torus, gear, frames. Threaded holes and nut pockets, M2 to M12. Fillets and chamfers with live preview. Cut by an adjustable plane. Measuring tool that snaps to edges and vertices.

Millimeters, centimeters or inches, your choice. A lock to keep a cylinder perfectly round. And when the part is ready, it goes to neoSlice in one click, with the right print settings.

Installs in one click from neoSlice. Windows and macOS.

Oen

Your local 3D printing expert.

An AI assistant that runs right on your computer, offline. No data sent anywhere. It knows your printer, your settings and the analysis of the current part, and helps with troubleshooting, calibration and maintenance, for every brand. A Thinking mode kicks in for tough questions.

AI Diagnostic

One photo. The defect. The fix.

Take a photo of a failed print. neoSlice identifies the defect, elephant's foot, stringing, under-extrusion, warping, layer shift, and suggests corrections to apply straight to your settings. Already know the defect? Pick it from the list, no photo needed.

Multicolor export

Your colors, counted to the gram.

After exporting a multicolor file, neoSlice calculates filament weight per color, colors the 3D preview, matches your spools and updates your stock automatically after printing.

Pro Workshop

Your workshop, under control.

From hobby to small business. Dashboard, spools, quotes based on real production cost, invoices that meet your country's rules, orders, customers and items. Everything is connected and saved automatically.
