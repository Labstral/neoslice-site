About

Every week, hundreds of messages come into the community. Beginners who just unboxed their printer, experienced makers looking to go faster. And in the middle of all that, always the same questions: why did my part break? Why did the supports rip everything off? What should I change?

Not for lack of skill. For lack of a tool that explains what it does.

I'm 32, passionate about computers and 3D printing. But what really drives me is helping people. That's why I've been running "Bambu Lab pour les nuls", a French Facebook group for Bambu Lab users, since December 13, 2025: 23,000+ members, hundreds of conversations a day. After answering the same questions over and over, I realized the problem wasn't the people. It was the interface between their intent and the machine.

So I started coding. Evenings, weekends. No funding, no team. Just the drive to solve this problem once and for all.

That's how neoSlice was born. You choose what you want, it takes care of the rest.
