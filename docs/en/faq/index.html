Answers to your questions

What is neoSlice?

neoSlice is an AI assistant that sets up your 3D prints for you. Choose your goal (strong, fast, precise): it analyzes the part and generates an optimal configuration, ready for 9 slicers (Bambu Studio, OrcaSlicer, PrusaSlicer and 6 others) and more than 600 printers.

Is neoSlice free?

Yes, neoSlice is free, with no time limit. The optional neoSlice Pro upgrade (one-time payment, for life) adds neoGen, the custom object generator, neoForge, the 3D modeler, Oen, the local AI assistant, photo diagnosis of failed prints, multicolor export and workshop management (quotes, invoices, customers, orders).

What is neoForge?

It's a 3D modeler, included in neoSlice Pro, for drawing a part when you can't find one that fits. You place a shape, stretch it with the mouse, drill holes, round the edges, take measurements, and the part goes straight into neoSlice, ready to print. It installs in one click from neoSlice and runs on Windows and Mac, just like the rest of the app.

Do I need technical knowledge?

No. That's the whole point of neoSlice: you choose your intent from simple menus, and neoSlice turns it into precise parameters. No expertise required.

Which printers are supported?

neoSlice supports more than 600 printers from all the major brands: Bambu Lab, Creality, Prusa, Anycubic, Voron, Elegoo, Sovol, Flashforge, Qidi… Select yours from the list: the settings adapt.

Do I need any other software?

Just your usual slicer, which you most likely already have. neoSlice doesn't replace it, it prepares for it: it analyzes the part, sets the parameters and exports a print-ready file. Works with 9 slicers (including Bambu Studio, OrcaSlicer and PrusaSlicer), covering more than 600 printers.

Which files can I import?

3MF, STL and OBJ files.

Are my files sent anywhere?

No. Your 3D models are analyzed locally on your computer and are never sent to any server. Only the photo diagnosis feature, in neoSlice Pro, shares images, and only if you agree to it. You can turn it off at any time in Settings.

Which systems does neoSlice run on?

Windows 10/11 (64-bit) and Macs with an Apple M2 chip or newer, running macOS 12 Monterey or later. M1 Macs and Intel Macs are not supported.

Is there a paid version?

The app is free. neoSlice Pro goes further: neoGen the object generator, neoForge the 3D modeler, Oen the local AI assistant, photo diagnosis and full workshop management (one-time payment, for life).
